# Xianxia video visual baseline

Apply these principles to every shot while allowing shot size and motion to change.

## Shared defaults

- Ratio: `16:9`
- Scale intensity: `史诗级`
- Saturation strategy: `选择性高饱和`
- Pace: `庄严舒缓`
- Adult figures: fictional unless authorized references are supplied

## Visual identity

1. Prove scale through people, repeated structures, bridges, trees, waterfalls, cloud layers, off-frame architecture, and extreme distance.
2. Use one dominant spatial geometry and one landmark per shot.
3. Preserve credible Eastern construction before adding carving and ornament.
4. Keep the image bright and color-separated while localizing high chroma.
5. Preserve near-neutral cloud highlights, pale stone, white jade, and moon white.
6. Use one motivated light direction and transparent cool shadows.
7. Keep foreground, midground, background, and extreme distance readable.
8. Make fantasy physics coherent: stable architecture, believable reflections, gravity-consistent cloth, and directional wind.

## Scale over time

Do not make every shot equally wide. Build scale through progression:

- Start with a partial reveal or known-size foreground.
- Establish the person in relation to the world.
- Show a travel axis or spatial connection.
- Escalate to the dominant landmark.
- End with a wider truth, destination, or emotional relationship.

For `超宏大`, ensure the sequence as a whole contains four scale bands even when an individual shot uses only two or three.

## Selective saturation over time

- Keep 65–75% of the frame in low-to-medium chroma sky, cloud, jade, stone, water, or neutral materials.
- Use 15–25% moderate chroma architecture and foliage.
- Limit the highest-chroma vermilion, coral, garment, flower, or gilded accents to roughly 5–10% by default.
- Keep warm light localized to exposed edges and planes; prohibit a global orange-gold wash.
- Preserve the palette across shots. Change saturation only when motivated by location, weather, or story beat.

## Video-specific visual failures

- Beautiful but unrelated shots: restore geography and continuity locks.
- Large buildings but weak grandeur: use known-size comparison, partial off-frame structures, extreme distance, and longer holds.
- Over-saturated montage: localize accents and preserve neutral clouds/jade across every shot.
- Generic motion: assign a movement that reveals new information.
- AI instability: simplify subject action, reduce compound camera motion, and repeat invariants.
